### Overview

I came across [Oriental Scale](https://www.mailleartisans.org/weaves/weavedisplay.php?key=794) by [ElementalDragon](https://www.mailleartisans.org/members/memberdisplay.php?key=6499) on [M.A.I.L.](https://www.mailleartisans.org/) while browsing for interesting weaves to try. This member of the [European]({{ site.baseurl }}{{ site.family_page }}#European), [Helm]({{ site.baseurl }}{{ site.family_page }}#Helm), and [Japanese]({{ site.baseurl }}{{ site.family_page }}#Japanese) weave families is an interesting unit weave similar to the [Aura 3 Unit]({{ site.baseurl }}{% post_url 2025-01-12-aura-3-unit %}). I followed [this tutorial](https://www.mailleartisans.org/articles/articledisplay.php?key=762) by [Chainmailbasket_com](https://www.mailleartisans.org/members/memberdisplay.php?key=75), and would recommend it to those who want to make the weave themselves.


### Materials

For the sample piece showcased in this post, I used two sizes of rings [made by hand]({{ site.baseurl }}{% post_url 2025-01-25-making-rings-at-home %}) from 16 {% include abbreviations/swg.html %} Bright Aluminum wire purchased from [The Ring Lord](https://theringlord.com/). The smaller rings have an {% include abbreviations/id.html %} of 5mm for an {% include abbreviations/ar.html %} of 3.1. The larger rings have an {% include abbreviations/id.html %} of 10mm for an {% include abbreviations/ar.html %} of 6.15.


### Notes

The Oriental Scale Unit is simple, both to understand and create. I find that it has a beautifully simple and elegant appearance. As a unit weave, it can be easily incorporated into earrings, bracelet or necklace charms, pendants, or even kept as a fun paperweight. While it isn’t very versatile, its ease of creation and aesthetic appeal make it worth learning, in my opinion.
