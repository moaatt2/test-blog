### Overview

While poking around the [ChainMaillers forum](https://chainmaillers.com/), I came across [Odin's Eye](https://chainmaillers.com/maillepedia/odins-eye.843/) by [Alix](https://www.mailleartisans.org/members/memberdisplay.php?key=19022). This item from the [European]({{ site.baseurl }}{{ site.family_page }}#European), [Japanese]({{ site.baseurl }}{{ site.family_page }}#Japanese), and [Helm]({{ site.baseurl }}{{ site.family_page }}#Helm) weave families is a simple variant of [Celtic Visions]({{ site.baseurl }}{% post_url 2024-08-11-celtic-visions %}) with the 'wings' crossed. If you wish to make this yourself, I recommend this [video tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0KaTRVvVTYI) by [Aussie Maille](https://www.youtube.com/@AussieMaillers).


### Materials

For the sample piece showcased in this post, I used two sizes of rings [made by hand]({{ site.baseurl }}{% post_url 2025-01-25-making-rings-at-home %}) from 16 {% include abbreviations/swg.html %} Bright Aluminum wire purchased from [The Ring Lord](https://theringlord.com/). The smaller rings have an {% include abbreviations/id.html %} of 7mm for an {% include abbreviations/ar.html %} of 4.3. The larger rings have an {% include abbreviations/id.html %} of 10mm for an {% include abbreviations/ar.html %} of 6.15.


### Notes

The Odin's Eye weave is not complex to understand and simple to create. In my opinion, the weave looks stunning. However, when flat, the wings may not always stick out sharply; you can mitigate this by placing the weave under tension. As a chain weave with a wide cross-section, it is well-suited for use in bracelets, chokers, and strapping. Given its usefulness, aesthetic appeal, and ease of learning and creation, I recommend learning how to make this weave.
